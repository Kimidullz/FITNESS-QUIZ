Food comparisons

Nasi putih vs Nasi goreng kampung

White rice is usually easier to control when losing weight. It saves about 400 kcal, and you can choose your own protein dishes.

Why?

  • A cup of white rice is about 200 kcal. A plate of nasi goreng kampung is about 600 kcal, which is 3 times more.
  • White rice has only about 1 g of fat. Nasi goreng kampung has about 24 g, mostly from cooking oil and fried ikan bilis.
  • Nasi goreng kampung has more protein (about 20 g vs 4 g). But white rice + a piece of chicken or fish can reach the same protein with fewer calories.
  • Fried rice is easy to eat fast and finish a full plate. White rice is easier to control, for example asking for half or just one cup.

When Nasi putih is still fine

White rice suits almost every day, especially at a nasi campur warung. Make sure you have a protein dish and vegetables.

When Nasi goreng kampung is still fine

Nasi goreng kampung is still okay once in a while, for example dinner after an active day or when you lepak with friends. Balance it with lighter meals at other times.

A smarter way

If you order nasi goreng kampung, ask for less oil, add a fried egg (not crispy) and vegetables. Then share it or pack half for tomorrow.

FAQ

Isn't white rice the reason people gain weight?

Not necessarily. What matters is your total calories for the day. A cup of rice with a protein dish and vegetables can fit into a weight-loss plan.

What if I still want nasi goreng?

You can. Choose a regular size, less oil, and add egg or chicken. Avoid adding keropok and sweet drinks in the same meal.
