Office Packed Lunch Ideas for Weight Loss (5 Days)

Bringing a packed lunch is one of the easiest ways to control portions without counting calories every day. You can still eat rice and Malaysian lauk. You just arrange your plate more wisely.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Lunchbox plate formula

A quarter of the box for rice or carbs, about 1 senduk of rice or roughly 150 g. A quarter for protein, about the size of your palm. The other half for vegetables. Add kuah and sambal just for taste, not poured on heavily.

1 hour of prep on the weekend

Cook 2 types of protein, for example chicken and fish, and keep them in the fridge. Wash and cut your vegetables in advance. Rice can be cooked every morning, or portioned out and frozen. In the morning you just reheat and pack.

Monday to Wednesday menu

Monday: rice, ayam masak kicap with less gravy and cabbage stir-fried with a little oil. Tuesday: rice, ikan kembung grilled or air-fried, a little sambal, cucumber and ulam. Wednesday: bihun goreng with egg and lots of vegetables, plus sliced chicken breast. Each one is an estimated 400 to 550 kcal depending on the oil and portion.

Thursday and Friday menu

Thursday: rice, sliced beef in black pepper sauce and broccoli or long beans. Friday: nasi goreng kampung using yesterday's rice, with egg, prawns, vegetables and a little ikan bilis. Both are also an estimated 400 to 550 kcal. Bring a piece of fruit for the afternoon so you're not too hungry before going home.

Store it safely

Cool cooked food quickly and keep it in the fridge. Finish it within 3 days and reheat until it's piping hot before eating. If your office has no fridge, use an insulated bag with an ice pack.

Try this week

  • Write a shopping list for these 5 menus before going to the market or supermarket.
  • Set aside 1 hour on Sunday to cook 2 types of protein and cut vegetables.
  • Buy a 3-compartment container so your rice, lauk and vegetable portions are clear.
  • Put one piece of fruit in your bag every morning for an afternoon snack.
Myth: Many think diet lunches must be salad and tasteless boiled chicken breast. Actually, Malaysian lauk like ayam kicap, ikan bakar and nasi goreng kampung are fine too, as long as you control the portion and oil.

FAQ

I don't have time to cook 5 days. What now?

Bringing lunch 2 or 3 days already helps. On other days, use the same plate formula at a nasi campur stall: a little rice, one protein lauk, lots of vegetables and a little kuah.

Can I bring white rice?

Yes. White rice is not the enemy. What matters is the portion and what you pair it with, which is protein and vegetables.

How many calories should my packed lunch have?

Everyone's needs are different. An estimated 400 to 550 kcal for lunch suits many women who are trying to lose weight. If you are pregnant or have a health condition like PCOS or thyroid problems, talk to a doctor or dietitian first.
