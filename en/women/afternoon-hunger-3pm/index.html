Super Hungry at 3-4pm: Why It Happens and How to Avoid It

Feeling very hungry in the afternoon is very common, and usually it's not because you lack discipline. Most of the time, it comes from what you ate for breakfast and lunch, your sleep and work stress.

Lunch is low in protein and vegetables

If your lunch is mostly rice or noodles with a little lauk, plus a sweet drink, your energy goes up fast and drops fast. Add protein like chicken, fish, eggs or tofu, and one or two types of vegetables. This combo keeps you full longer.

Skipping breakfast or eating too little

Many people skip breakfast because they're rushing and stuck in traffic. That's okay if you're really not hungry in the morning, but notice if you get very hungry in the afternoon. A simple breakfast like boiled eggs with bread, or half a nasi lemak with egg, can help.

Not enough sleep and not enough water

When you don't sleep enough, your body tends to look for sweet, quick-energy food. Thirst can also sometimes feel like hunger. Drink a glass of water first and wait 10 minutes before deciding if you're really hungry.

Plan an afternoon snack

It's better to eat a small planned snack at 3pm than to hold your hunger until you attack the kuih or overeat when you get home. For example, fruit with a small handful of nuts, plain yogurt or a boiled egg. Snacks like these are an estimated 100-200 kcal.

When to see a doctor

If your hunger is extreme, and comes with dizziness, shaking, sweating, frequent thirst or going to the toilet often, see a doctor for a check-up. This is also important if you have PCOS, a thyroid problem or are pregnant.

Try this week

  • Add one protein source to your lunch every day this week.
  • Bring one planned afternoon snack from home.
  • Drink a glass of water every time you feel hungry before 3pm.
  • Get up and walk for 5 minutes after lunch or when you feel sleepy.
  • Try sleeping 30 minutes earlier on at least 3 nights.
Myth: Some people believe feeling hungry means fat is burning, so the longer you hold it, the better. Actually, extreme hunger usually makes you overeat later, so planned eating helps more in the long run.

FAQ

Why do I crave sweet things every afternoon?

Usually because your lunch wasn't filling enough, you lack sleep, or your energy drops when work gets tiring. Try improving your lunch and prepare a planned snack. If you still want something sweet, have a little and enjoy it.

Can drinking coffee hold off hunger?

Coffee can help for a while, but it doesn't replace food. If your coffee is sweet and creamy, it also adds calories. Kopi o kurang manis with a protein snack is more balanced.

I'm pregnant and always hungry in the afternoon. Are these tips suitable?

During pregnancy, your food needs are different and it's not the time to aim for weight loss. Discuss a suitable eating pattern with your doctor.
