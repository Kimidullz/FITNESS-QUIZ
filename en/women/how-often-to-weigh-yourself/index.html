Fat loss for working women

How Often Should You Weigh Yourself? Daily, Weekly or Monthly

For most working women, weighing once a week, or 2-3 times a week at the same time, is enough. What matters is not how often you weigh, but that you look at the trend, not one number.

Why your weight goes up and down every day

Your body weight changes every day because of water, salt, carbs and what's in your gut. After a nasi lemak with lots of sambal or a salty mamak mee goreng, your weight can go up 0.5-1.5 kg the next day. That's mostly water, not fat. Normal, don't panic.

Weighing daily: who is it for?

Weighing every day suits you if you can look at the number calmly. Record it daily, then work out the weekly average. If the ups and downs spoil your mood for the whole day at the office, this method is not for you.

Weighing weekly: the easiest way

Pick one fixed day, for example Wednesday or Thursday. Avoid Monday because your body is still holding water from weekend eating. Weigh in the morning, after going to the toilet, before eating and drinking, in light clothing.

Take your menstrual cycle into account

Many women gain weight temporarily a few days before and during their period. So compare your weight with the same week last month. That's fairer than comparing week to week.

The scale is not the only measure

Measure your waist, take photos and notice how your work clothes fit. Sometimes your weight doesn't change much, but your slacks are already looser. That's still progress.

Try this week

  • Pick one fixed day and time to weigh this week, and set a reminder on your phone.
  • Make a note on your phone to record your weight, and mark your period days too.
  • Measure your waist at belly button level with a measuring tape, and repeat every 2-4 weeks.
  • Take front and side photos in the same clothes, and repeat every 2 weeks.
Myth: Many people believe gaining 1 kg overnight means gaining 1 kg of fat. In reality, to add 1 kg of fat you need to overeat by an estimated 7,000 kcal. An overnight jump is almost always water and gut content.

FAQ

My weight went up 1 kg after a wedding kenduri. Is my diet ruined?

No. Kenduri food is usually salty and high in carbs, so your body holds more water. Go back to your usual routine, drink enough water, and your weight usually settles again within 2-4 days.

Can I trust the body fat percentage reading on a digital scale?

That reading is only an estimate and changes easily with the water in your body. Use it to see long-term trends, not as an exact number. Waist measurement is often more useful.

How long before I see my weight go down?

A realistic rate is an estimated 0.5-1 kg a week. Judge the trend over 2-4 weeks, not 2-3 days. Some weeks it drops a lot, some weeks it seems like it's not moving at all.
