Fat loss for working women

Drinking Enough? Plain Water, False Hunger and Body Weight

Many working women drink less plain water than they think, especially when busy with meetings and drinking more teh tarik. Plain water doesn't magically burn fat. But it helps you tell thirst apart from hunger, and cuts calories from sweet drinks.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

How much is enough?

A general guide for most adults is an estimated 6-8 glasses a day (1.5-2 litres). You need more when the weather is hot, you work outdoors or you exercise. Pale yellow urine is usually a sign your intake is okay. If you have heart or kidney problems, follow your doctor's advice.

False hunger

Sometimes thirst feels like hunger, especially in the afternoon at the office. Drink a glass of water and wait 10-15 minutes. If you're still hungry, have your planned snack.

Hidden calories in drinks

A glass of teh tarik or kopi susu can reach an estimated 150-200 kcal. Two glasses a day is almost the same as a small meal. At the mamak, try ordering teh o kosong, kopi kurang manis or warm water.

Water and the number on the scale

Your weight can go up temporarily after eating salty food like nasi lemak or instant noodles. That's water, not fat. Drink as usual, cut down on salty food the next day, and look at your weight trend over a few weeks.

Fasting month

While fasting, spread out your water intake between buka puasa and sahur, don't gulp it all at once. For religious questions, refer to an ustazah. If you have a specific health condition, check with your doctor before fasting.

Try this week

  • Put a 1-litre water bottle on your work desk, and aim to finish it twice a day.
  • Drink a glass of water before every meal.
  • Swap one daily sweet drink for plain water, teh o kosong or a less sweet option.
  • Set 3 reminders on your phone to drink water during busy hours.
  • Add slices of limau nipis or cucumber if you're bored of plain water.
Myth: Many people believe drinking lots of water can 'flush' fat out of the body. In reality, water doesn't remove fat. Fat comes down when you're consistently in a calorie deficit, while water helps your body function and control appetite.

FAQ

Do coffee and tea count as drinking water?

Yes, they add to your total water intake. But watch the sugar and condensed milk, because that's where the calories add up. Make plain water your main drink.

Does drinking cold water or ice make you fat?

No. Cold water has no calories. What adds calories is the sugar and milk in iced drinks, not the ice.

Can I drink too much water?

It's rare for ordinary people, but there's no need to force yourself to drink litres and litres. Drink according to your thirst and daily needs. If you have health problems, ask your doctor.
