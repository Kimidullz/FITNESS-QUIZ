Fat loss for working women

In Your 30s, Has Your Metabolism Slowed Down? The Real Facts

The good news is, a large study found that metabolic rate is fairly stable from your 20s to around age 60, after accounting for body size. What usually changes in your 30s is lifestyle: moving less, sleeping less, more stress and more 'in passing' eating.

What actually changes

Work gets busier, meetings get longer and you spend more time stuck in traffic. If you WFH, your daily steps may not even reach 3,000. Meeting kuih, sweet afternoon coffee and late-night eating also become more frequent. All of this adds calories and reduces movement, even though your metabolism hasn't changed much.

Muscle decreases if you don't train it

If you don't do strength training, your muscle can decrease little by little year by year. Less muscle means your body feels less strong and your body shape changes too. Strength training 2-3 times a week, even using just your own body weight, helps maintain it.

Daily movement matters more than you think

Calories burned through daily movement like walking, taking the stairs and tidying the house can differ a lot from person to person. Get up every hour, take calls while walking and park a little further away. These small habits are easy to fit into a busy day.

Sleep and stress

Sleeping late because of work or scrolling your phone can make you hungrier and more prone to cravings the next day. Stress also makes many people eat without noticing. Try setting a more consistent bedtime. It helps your energy and your food choices too.

When to see a doctor

If you feel extremely tired, lose a lot of hair, often feel cold or gain weight without any lifestyle change, see a doctor. A doctor can check your thyroid and other things through blood tests. In your late 30s to 40s, if your period pattern changes, discuss it with a doctor too.

Try this week

  • Do 2 strength training sessions of 20 minutes this week, for example squats, lunges and push-ups.
  • Set a phone reminder to get up and walk for 2-3 minutes every hour during work.
  • Limit meeting kuih to just one piece, or keep it to share.
  • Try going to bed 30 minutes earlier at least 3 nights this week.
  • Make sure every main meal has protein, for example chicken, fish, eggs or tofu.
Myth: Many believe that after 30 you just can't lose weight anymore. In reality, many women in their 30s and 40s successfully lose fat with a small deficit, strength training and consistent habits.

FAQ

Why could I eat a lot before without gaining weight?

In their 20s, many people moved more, slept earlier and didn't do much 'in passing' eating at the office. It's mostly the lifestyle that changed, not just metabolism.

Do I need to eat very little to lose weight now?

No need. Eating too little makes you tired and makes it hard to stay consistent. A small deficit that leads to a loss of an estimated 0.5-1 kg a week is usually easier to maintain.

Cardio or strength training, which is better?

Both have benefits. If time is limited, prioritise strength training 2-3 times a week and add walking every day.
