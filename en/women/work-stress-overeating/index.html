Fat loss for working women

Work stress makes you eat a lot: how to break the cycle

If you feel like eating every time work gets intense, you are not alone and this is not a sign of weakness. With a few small steps, you can cut down stress eating without giving up your favourite foods.

Why stress makes us want to eat

When stressed, your body and brain look for a quick way to feel relief. Sweet, fatty and crunchy foods do give a short moment of comfort. The problem is the relief is brief, and this cycle repeats every tough workday.

Know your triggers

Notice when the urge to eat comes. After a tense meeting? After replying to your boss's email? While stuck in traffic? Once you know your triggers, you can prepare in advance instead of reacting automatically.

Don't skip lunch

Many working women skip lunch because they are busy, then get starving and eat a lot in the afternoon or at night. Have a lunch with protein like chicken, fish, eggs or tofu, with vegetables and enough rice. A properly filled stomach is easier to manage when stress hits.

Prepare your own snacks

Keep snacks you like but that are more filling in your office drawer. For example, nuts in a small container, fruit, yogurt or boiled eggs. When the meeting kuih arrives, you can calmly take one because you are not too hungry.

If you've already overeaten

No need to feel guilty or 'punish' yourself by not eating the next day. One day of overeating does not ruin your progress. Just eat normally at your next meal and get back to your routine.

Try this week

  • Write down the 3 work situations that most often make you want to eat, in your phone notes.
  • Make sure you eat lunch every workday this week, even if it's just 15 minutes.
  • Bring one protein snack to the office for the afternoon.
  • When you feel like stress eating, wait 10 minutes first while drinking plain water or taking a short walk.
  • Take 5 deep breaths before opening a snack pack.
Myth: Many think stress eating is just a lack of discipline. In fact, it is the body's normal response to pressure, and it is easier to manage with meal planning and rest, not by being angry at yourself.

FAQ

Can I eat chocolate when stressed?

Yes. Take a small portion, eat slowly and enjoy it. What matters is how often and how much, not banning it completely.

What if stress makes me lose my appetite completely?

Try eating small but frequent portions, like eggs, bread or fruit. If your appetite stays gone for a long time or you lose weight for no reason, please see a doctor.

When should I get professional help?

If you feel your eating is out of control, often eat until it hurts, or stress is affecting your sleep and work, talk to a doctor or counsellor. Asking for help is a wise step.
