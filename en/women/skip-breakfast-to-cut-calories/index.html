Fat loss for working women

Skipping breakfast to cut calories: good idea or not?

Skipping breakfast can help some women, but it's not a must for losing weight. What matters most is your total calories for the whole day, not the time you start eating.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

What actually counts

You lose weight when the calories you take in are slightly less than what your body uses. If you skip breakfast but eat meeting kuih in the afternoon and eat more at night, your calories stay the same or even higher. So the question isn't 'breakfast or no breakfast', but 'which one makes you eat less overall'.

When skipping breakfast suits you

If you're really not hungry in the morning and can last until lunch without feeling dizzy, it may suit you. Many working women find it easier to control their eating when their 'eating window' is shorter. Make sure your lunch has enough protein and vegetables so you don't get bad cravings after that.

When it becomes a problem

If skipping breakfast makes you super hungry at 3 pm, quick-tempered, or 'revenge eating' at night, this approach doesn't suit you. This is very common when you work shifts or have long meetings. It's better to have a simple, high-protein breakfast.

Easy simple breakfasts

Two hard-boiled eggs with a slice of bread, an estimated 250-300 kcal. Or one plain roti canai with less-sweet tea and a little dhal curry. Nasi lemak is fine too, just pick a small packet and add an egg, and go easy on the oily sambal.

Health conditions

If you have diabetes, gastric problems, PCOS, thyroid problems or are pregnant, don't change your meal times without asking your doctor first. Every body has different needs.

Try this week

  • For 3 days, note down whether you feel hungry in the morning or not.
  • Try one week with a high-protein breakfast and one week without breakfast, then compare your afternoon hunger.
  • Prepare 4-6 hard-boiled eggs on the weekend for quick breakfasts.
  • If you skip breakfast, drink plain water or less-sweet coffee, not full-sugar teh tarik.
Myth: Many people believe skipping breakfast damages your metabolism. Actually, there's no strong evidence for that; what has more impact is your total calories and food quality throughout the day.

FAQ

If I skip breakfast, can I still drink milk coffee in the morning?

Yes, but sweet milk coffee has calories, an estimated 100-180 kcal per cup. Choose less sweet or kopi O with less sugar if you want to save calories.

I work night shifts, when is my 'breakfast'?

Count breakfast as the first meal after you wake up, no matter what time it is. Focus on how much you eat in 24 hours and try to keep consistent meal times.

Is skipping breakfast the same as intermittent fasting?

More or less, because it shortens your eating window. But it still only works if your total calories go down. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor first.
