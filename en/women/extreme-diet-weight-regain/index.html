Extreme Diets and 'Lose 5 kg in a Week': Why Does the Weight Come Back?

Most of the weight lost in a week of extreme dieting is water, not fat. When you go back to eating normally, that water returns and your weight goes back up.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

What you're actually losing

When you eat very little or cut carbs completely, your body loses water and stored energy in your muscles. One kilo of fat equals an estimated 7,700 calories. So it's almost impossible to lose 5 kg of fat in a week.

Why the weight comes back

Water and stored energy return as soon as you eat rice again. Hunger that's been held back for a long time often makes you overeat. Old habits also come back because the diet can't be continued.

Effects on your work and body

A diet that's too strict can make you tired, dizzy, short-tempered and unable to focus in meetings. Some women get irregular periods. If this happens, stop and see a doctor.

A more realistic way

Aim for around 0.5-1 kg a week, or slower. Reduce your portions a little, add protein and vegetables, and move more. Slow but lasting is better than fast but coming back.

Try this week

  • Pick one small habit to change this week, for example cutting one sweet drink a day.
  • Eat three meals that include protein. Don't skip meals until you're starving.
  • Walk for 10 minutes after lunch.
  • Set a 4-8 week target, not 7 days.
Myth: Many believe losing weight fast means the diet worked. In truth, success means the weight you lose stays off, and that usually comes from slow changes.

FAQ

I have a wedding next month. Can I go on a strict diet?

In a month, you can still see changes with a moderate deficit, less salt and fewer sweet drinks, and enough sleep. Avoid starvation diets that leave you exhausted on the day of the event.

Can I do intermittent fasting?

Yes, if it fits your schedule and you don't overeat when you break your fast. It's just one way to arrange your meal times, not magic. If you have gastric problems, diabetes or are pregnant, check with your doctor first.

Is my metabolism damaged because of past diets?

Metabolism can slow down a little after a strict diet, but it's usually not permanently damaged. By eating enough protein and lifting weights, your body can recover gradually.
