Eating Lunch Out but Want to Lose Weight: How to Choose

Yes, you can still eat lunch out and lose weight. The key isn't avoiding the shops, but knowing how to arrange your plate and choose your drink.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Use a simple plate formula

Imagine your plate divided into parts. Half vegetables, a quarter protein like chicken, fish, eggs or tofu, and a quarter rice. At a nasi campur stall, ask for just one scoop of rice. One scoop of white rice is an estimated 150-200 kcal, so it can still fit into your daily budget.

Watch out for flooded gravy

Curry gravy, santan gravy and frying oil can add an estimated 100-200 kcal without you noticing. Ask for the gravy on the side or just a little. Choose grilled, steamed, soup-based or asam dishes when available. If you want fried lauk, take just one and add vegetables.

Even at the mamak there are options

Nasi kandar is fine, just choose one protein lauk and ask for less gravy. One piece of roti canai is an estimated 300 kcal, so one piece with dal and a boiled egg is more filling than two plain pieces. Tosai, chapati or chicken soup with half rice are also okay options.

Drinks are often overlooked

A glass of teh tarik or sirap ais can be an estimated 150-200 kcal. If you drink it every day, it adds up a lot in a week. Try switching to teh o kosong, kopi o kurang manis or warm water. No need to cut it out right away, start with less sugar first.

Eating with office friends

You don't need to be the odd one out or bring salad every day. Go to the same shop, just change how you take your lauk. Eat slowly and chat, so your brain has time to feel full.

Try this week

  • This week, ask for one scoop of rice every time you eat nasi campur.
  • Add one type of vegetable to your plate at every lunch.
  • Switch sweet drinks to less sweet or no sugar on at least 3 days.
  • Ask for gravy on the side or less gravy at your usual shop.
  • Take a photo of your lunch plate for 5 days to see your own eating pattern.
Myth: Many think losing weight means eating only salad every day. Actually, nasi campur arranged properly, with protein and vegetables, can help you lose weight and is easier to keep up.

FAQ

Can I eat rice every day if I want to lose weight?

Yes. What matters is the portion size and what you eat with it. One scoop of rice with protein and vegetables is very different from a full plate of rice with flooded gravy.

How fast can I see results?

A realistic target is an estimated 0.5-1 kg a week, and everyone is different. Weight also goes up and down daily because of water and hormones, so look at the trend over a few weeks.

I have PCOS or a thyroid problem. Are these tips suitable?

The balanced plate principle usually suits many people. But if you have PCOS, a thyroid problem or are pregnant, discuss with your doctor first for guidance that suits your condition.
