Fat loss for working women

Coffee, teh tarik and office sweet drinks: how many calories are you really drinking

Sweet drinks can add hundreds of calories a day without you noticing, because they don't fill you up like food does. You don't have to stop completely. Just cut down the sugar, the size and how often you have them.

Estimated calories in popular drinks

These are estimates for one regular glass at a mamak or café. Teh tarik or kopi susu: 150 to 200 kcal. Sirap bandung: 180 to 220 kcal. Teh O ais: 80 to 100 kcal. 3-in-1 coffee, one sachet: 100 to 120 kcal. Kopi O kosong: under 10 kcal. Regular-size bubble tea: 300 to 450 kcal.

Why it's easy to overdo it

Drinks don't fill you up like rice or lauk. So after a glass of teh tarik, you still eat as usual. If you have 2 glasses of teh tarik a day, that's an estimated 300 to 400 kcal, or up to 2,000 kcal over one work week.

Swap without suffering

Start by ordering 'kurang manis' (less sweet), then 'kosong' (no sugar) once your taste buds get used to it. Choose a small size, or hot instead of iced, which is usually bigger. Save your full teh tarik for one lepak session you really enjoy.

Drinks at meetings and the pantry

The office pantry usually has 3-in-1 sachets that are easy to grab. Try bringing your own coffee powder or tea bags and add a little milk. At meetings, take one cup and refill it with plain water.

Caffeine and your health

Most adults are okay with 2 to 3 cups of coffee a day. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, diabetes or get palpitations easily, ask your doctor what limit suits you.

Try this week

  • Count how many sweet drinks you have today, without changing anything yet.
  • Switch one drink a day to 'kurang manis' this week.
  • Bring a water bottle to your desk and finish it at least once before lunch.
  • Replace one bubble tea this week with teh O kurang manis or kopi O.
Myth: Many people think drinks don't count because they're not food. In reality, calories from sweet drinks still go into your daily total. They just don't make you feel full.

FAQ

Are artificial sweeteners okay?

For some people, they can help cut down sugar gradually. If you are pregnant or have a health condition like diabetes, talk to your doctor first.

Is fresh fruit juice better than sweet drinks?

Juice has vitamins, but its calories are still high, an estimated 100 to 150 kcal per glass. Eating whole fruit is usually more filling.

I can't live without teh tarik. What do I do?

You don't need to stop. Cut down to one glass a day, choose kurang manis, and really enjoy it. That on its own is already a meaningful change.
