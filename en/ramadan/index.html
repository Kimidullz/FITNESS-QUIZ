Ramadan Guide: Fasting Without Gaining Weight

Ramadan is a month of worship, not a month of strict dieting. But many people are shocked when they gain 2-4 kg after Raya. In this guide, Coach Kimi and Coach Zara share realistic sahur and berbuka menus, estimated calories for bazar food and kuih raya, plus easy tips for working adults and mums. No need to give up rice. No need to avoid the bazar. Just choose your portions and frequency wisely. All calorie and protein numbers are estimates for a typical serving in Malaysia. Actual sizes can differ by shop and recipe. Important: if you have diabetes, are pregnant or breastfeeding, please talk to your doctor first before changing how you fast or eat.

Baca dalam BM: Panduan Ramadan

Why many people gain weight while fasting

  • 'Revenge' berbuka. Hungry the whole day, so we eat fast and a lot in one go. Add fried food and sweet drinks, and berbuka alone can go over 1,500 kcal.
  • Liquid calories and night snacks are not counted. Air bandung, air tebu, cendol, moreh after terawih and kuih while watching TV. Each one looks small, but added together it really adds up.
  • Less sleep and less movement. Waking up for sahur, sleeping late, the body is tired, so we walk and exercise less. When we sleep less, our appetite usually goes up too.

Bubur lambuk is a Ramadan tradition that is gentle on the stomach. Add ayam bakar so you get enough protein.

Exercising while fasting

The best time is 30-60 minutes before berbuka (for example brisk walking or light exercise), or 1-2 hours after berbuka for heavier exercise like weight training. Lower the intensity compared to usual and keep sessions short, just 20-40 minutes. If you feel dizzy, your heart is racing or you feel too weak, stop and rest. Besides exercise, stay active through the day: walking to the surau for terawih and taking the stairs at the office also count.

Tips to fast without gaining weight

  • Break your fast with kurma and plain water first. Pray Maghrib, then only eat rice. This 10-minute break helps you avoid overeating.
  • Use the plate formula: half vegetables, quarter rice, quarter protein like chicken, fish, eggs or tauhu.
  • Go to the bazar with a list. Choose just 1 main dish and 1 kuih. Avoid shopping when you are too hungry.
  • Swap sweet drinks for plain water, teh O kurang manis or plain water with lime slices. Save air bandung and air tebu for 2-3 times a week.
  • Don't skip sahur. A sahur with protein helps control hunger and prevents overeating at berbuka.
  • Drink water little by little from berbuka until sahur. Keep a water bottle on the table so it's easy to remember.
  • Moreh after terawih? Take 1 small piece or just fruit. It doesn't need to be a second dinner.
  • Try to get at least 6 hours of sleep per night, for example sleep early after terawih and take a short nap during your lunch break if you can.

Raya open houses without the guilt

  • Eat something with protein before going out to beraya, for example a boiled egg. With a stomach that's not empty, it's easier to control portions.
  • At each house, take just 1 small plate. Choose the dishes you like most, no need to taste everything.
  • Kuih raya: pick 2-3 favourite pieces, put them on a small plate, then move away from the kuih table. Avoid eating straight from the jar.
  • For air sirap and sweet drinks, you can ask for half a glass, or ask for plain water. Hosts usually understand.
  • Raya is not just one day, so plan ahead. When you have an evening invitation, eat light at lunch and go for a walk after eating with the family.

FAQ

Can I exercise while fasting?

Yes, for most healthy adults. Choose light exercise before berbuka or heavier exercise after berbuka. Listen to your body. If you feel dizzy or too weak, stop and rest.

How much water should I drink?

A general guide is around 8 glasses from berbuka to sahur. For example 2 glasses at berbuka, 4 glasses between terawih and bedtime, 2 glasses at sahur. Everyone's needs are different, so watch the colour of your urine. Light yellow is usually a sign you're okay.

Kurma is high in sugar, can I eat it?

Yes. Roughly 20 kcal per small kurma. 2-3 kurma at berbuka is suitable to raise your energy quickly. What you need to watch is eating handful after handful throughout the night.

If I skip sahur, I eat fewer calories, so will I lose weight faster?

Not necessarily. Many people who skip sahur end up too hungry and overeat at berbuka. A simple sahur with protein helps you control your appetite better.

Does eating rice at night make you fat?

No. Body weight is affected by total calories throughout the day, not the time you eat rice. 1 scoop of rice with a protein dish and vegetables can fit into your plan.

I have diabetes, or I'm pregnant or breastfeeding. Can I follow this guide?

Please see your doctor first before changing how you fast or eat. Your blood sugar, medication and nutrition needs are different, so your doctor's advice comes first. This guide is general information only.
