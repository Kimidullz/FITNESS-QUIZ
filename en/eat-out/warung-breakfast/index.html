Eating out

What to order at a breakfast warung when you want to lose weight

Breakfast at the warung is a daily routine for many Malaysians. Nasi lemak, roti canai, mee goreng, kuih. You can eat all of them. There are just some options that keep you full longer with more moderate calories. Focus on protein like eggs, control the oil and sugar, and watch your sweet drinks. All numbers are estimates for a typical warung serving.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Best picks

~150 kcal2 half-boiled eggs
High protein and cheap. Keeps you full until lunch.
~250 kcalKaya toast, a little butter (2 slices)
Lighter than roti canai. Pair it with half-boiled eggs for a complete breakfast.
~400 kcalPlain nasi lemak bungkus with boiled egg
Yes, nasi lemak is okay! A small packet with boiled egg is more moderate than the fried chicken version.
~380 kcalSmall packet of bihun goreng with egg
Bihun goreng is usually less oily than mamak-style mee goreng. Take a small portion.
~300 kcal1 roti canai kosong with dhal
If you're craving roti canai, one piece with dhal is lighter than two pieces with curry.
~180 kcal1 small pau kacang or apam balik
Steamed kuih is lighter than fried kuih. Good if you want something a little sweet.

Be careful with these

~800 kcalNasi lemak with fried chicken
Santan rice, fried chicken and oily sambal. Very tasty, but the calories are almost half of what many people need in a day.
~650 kcal2 roti canai with mixed curry
Roti canai is kneaded with oil and margarine. The curry adds more fat.
~650 kcalFull plate of mamak mee goreng
Lots of oil and sauce. Warung portions are usually big.
~400 kcalKaripap, cucur or pisang goreng (3 pieces)
Fried kuih soak up oil. Easy to take 'one more' while sipping tea.
~150 kcalRegular teh tarik or kopi susu
Sweetened condensed milk and sugar. If you drink two glasses every morning, the calories add up.

How to order

  • Add a half-boiled or boiled egg to your breakfast. Protein keeps you from getting hungry quickly.
  • When ordering nasi lemak: 'nasi sikit, sambal asing' (less rice, sambal on the side). Take as much sambal as you need for taste.
  • Roti canai: choose dhal, ask for the gravy on the side, and stick to one piece.
  • Kuih: choose just one, and go for steamed kuih like pau or putu first.
  • Drinks: ask for 'kurang manis' (less sweet) or 'kosong' (no sugar). Teh o kosong and kopi o kosong have almost no calories.

Drinks

Kopi O kosong or teh O kosong (black coffee or tea, no sugar). If you need milk, a small teh tarik kurang manis (less sweet).

FAQ

I can't give up nasi lemak. What should I do?

No need to give it up. Choose a plain packet with egg, ask for less rice and sambal on the side. Have it 2-3 times a week, not every day. On other days, try toast with eggs.

Is it okay if I skip breakfast completely?

It depends on what feels comfortable for you. Some people are fine skipping it, others get too hungry and overeat at lunch. What matters is your total food for the day. If you often 'take revenge' at lunch, a light breakfast with protein may suit you better.
