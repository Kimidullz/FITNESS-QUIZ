Eating out

What to order at pizza places (Domino's, Pizza Hut) when you want to lose weight

Friday night, the family wants pizza. You don't need to be the one eating salad alone. Pizza can fit your plan if you choose your crust, toppings and number of slices wisely. The calories below are estimates for regular or medium size. Actual values differ by shop and topping.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Best picks

~380 kcal2 slices thin crust chicken pizza (medium)
Thin crust has less flour and oil. Chicken topping gives protein.
~340 kcal2 slices thin crust veggie pizza
Light with lots of vegetables. Good if you want to add wings or soup.
~440 kcal2 slices regular crust chicken pizza (hand tossed)
If there's no thin crust, this is an okay middle option.
~300 kcalBaked wings, 4 pieces
High protein. Eat with 1-2 slices of pizza to feel fuller.
~170 kcalMushroom soup, 1 bowl
Warm and filling. Have it first before pizza so you don't overdo the slices.
~120 kcalGarden salad (if available), dressing on the side
Adds fibre and fills you up. Use only half the dressing.

Be careful with these

~700 kcal2 slices cheese stuffed crust pizza
The cheese in the crust adds a lot of calories. Tasty, but count it properly.
~620 kcal2 slices pan pizza with meat/pepperoni
Thick oily crust and processed meat. Calories go up fast.
~350 kcalGarlic bread, 4 pieces
Butter and bread. People often eat it before the pizza arrives, then overeat.
~700 kcalCreamy baked pasta (1 serving)
Cream sauce and cheese. Same calories as 3-4 slices of pizza.
~350 kcalChocolate lava cake, 1 piece
Calorie-dense dessert. Share between two if you want a taste.

How to order

  • Decide from the start: just 2 slices. Put them on a plate, don't eat straight from the box.
  • Pick thin crust or regular crust. Avoid cheese crust and pan if you're focused on losing weight.
  • Add vegetable toppings like mushroom, capsicum, onion. Saves calories and adds volume.
  • Start with soup or salad first, then take pizza. Your stomach is already half full.
  • Order to share, not individual sets. Easier to control and you won't feel you have to finish it all.

Drinks

Plain water or sugar-free fizzy drinks. One large glass of sweet drink can add 200 kcal or more.

FAQ

If I'm having pizza, should I skip lunch?

No need. Skipping meals usually makes you hungrier and you eat more that night. Have lunch as usual, just reduce your rice a little if you want to make room.

What if I end up eating 4-5 slices?

It's okay, one night won't ruin your progress. Tomorrow, continue eating as usual, drink enough water and keep walking. What matters is consistency week after week.
