Walking for weight loss: how many steps a day?

No need to chase 10,000 steps straight away. Start by adding 1,000 to 2,000 steps to your current average, and aim for an estimated 7,000 to 10,000 steps a day step by step.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Works: Legs, glutes, calves and core, plus heart health. Walking adds to your daily energy burn, but weight loss still depends on eating too.
For beginners: Start with your current average plus 1,000 steps. Beginner target: an estimated 7,000-8,000 steps a day, 5-7 days a week, then go up to 10,000 if comfortable.

How to do it

  1. Check your average daily steps for a week using your phone or watch.
  2. Add 1,000 steps a day to that average. Increase again every 1-2 weeks.
  3. Walk for 10 minutes after meals, for example after lunch at the mamak.
  4. Park further away, take the stairs, and walk while you're on the phone.
  5. Once in a while, walk fast enough that you can talk but can't sing.

Common mistakes

  • Jumping straight from 3,000 to 12,000 steps and ending up with sore feet or giving up.
  • Feeling like you've walked a lot, so you eat bigger portions or drink more sweet drinks than usual.
  • Wearing unsuitable footwear like thin slippers for long walks.

Easier version

Break it into 3-4 short walks of 5-10 minutes throughout the day.

Harder version

Add hills or stairs, walk faster, or do a 30-45 minute walk at the park on weekends.

FAQ

Do I really need exactly 10,000 steps?

No. 10,000 isn't a magic number. Consistently adding steps from where you are now matters more than chasing one number.

How many calories do 1,000 steps burn?

A rough estimate is 30-50 calories, depending on body weight and speed. It looks small, but it adds up every day.

Is walking alone enough to lose weight?

It's a great start. Combine it with suitable rice portions, more vegetables and protein, and strength training 2 times a week for more balanced results.
