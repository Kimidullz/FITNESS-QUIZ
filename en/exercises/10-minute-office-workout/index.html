10-minute office workout

Sitting long hours at the office makes your body stiff and you move less. This 10-minute routine can be done next to your desk, without changing clothes and without sweating much.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Works: Light full-body work: legs, glutes, chest, arms, shoulders and back. It also helps boost blood flow.
For beginners: 10 minutes, 1 to 2 times a day on workdays. On top of that, get up and move for 2-3 minutes every hour.

How to do it

  1. Minutes 1-2: Walk on the spot or to the pantry and back, swinging your arms.
  2. Minutes 3-4: Squat to a chair, 10 times, rest a bit, repeat.
  3. Minutes 5-6: Desk push-ups (make sure the desk is sturdy), 10 times, repeat.
  4. Minutes 7-8: Calf raises 15 times, then roll your shoulders backwards 10 times.
  5. Minutes 9-10: Chest stretch in a door frame and a slow seated twist on your chair, 30 seconds each side.

Common mistakes

  • Using a chair with wheels for squats or a wobbly desk for push-ups.
  • Going too fast until you're out of breath and sweaty before a meeting.
  • Holding your breath. Breathe normally throughout each move.

Easier version

Do every move halfway only, or pick 2-3 moves for 5 minutes.

Harder version

Repeat the routine twice a day, or increase squats and push-ups up to 15 reps.

FAQ

I'm shy to do this in front of colleagues. What to do?

Do it in the pantry, the stairwell or an empty room. Calf raises and shoulder rolls can even be done while standing at the printer without anyone noticing.

Is 10 minutes enough to lose weight?

It helps add to your daily movement, and every bit of movement counts. But weight loss depends more on your food portions and overall activity across the week.

When is the best time to do it?

After lunch or when you feel sleepy in the afternoon works well. Choose a time that's easy for you to repeat every day.
