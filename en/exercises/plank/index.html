How to Do a Proper Plank for Beginners (At Home, No Equipment)

The plank is a hold exercise that strengthens your core, shoulders and glutes. All you need is the floor, a mat or carpet, and you can start with just 15 seconds.

Read in Malay: versi Bahasa Melayu

Works: Core muscles (front abs, sides and lower back), shoulders, chest and glutes. Planks strengthen your abs, but they don't specifically burn tummy fat. Fat goes down from your whole body through eating and daily movement.
For beginners: Beginner: 3 sets x 15-30 seconds, rest 30-60 seconds between sets. Do it 3 times a week. Add 5 seconds every week when it feels easy.

How to do it

  1. Place your elbows directly under your shoulders, forearms flat on the floor.
  2. Straighten your legs behind you and balance on your toes.
  3. Squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button in slightly.
  4. Keep your head, back and heels in one straight line.
  5. Look at the floor slightly ahead so your neck stays neutral.
  6. Breathe slowly and hold for the set time.

Common mistakes

  • Hips dropping. This puts pressure on your lower back. Squeeze your glutes and tummy to lift back up.
  • Hips too high like a triangle. Lower them until your body is straight.
  • Holding your breath. Keep breathing slowly through your nose and mouth.

Easier version

Do a knee plank (knees on the floor, body straight from head to knees). Or do a plank leaning on a wall, dining table or sofa.

Harder version

Lift one leg for 2-3 seconds, alternating. Or do a high plank (straight arms) while tapping your shoulders alternately (shoulder tap).

FAQ

Can planks shrink a big tummy?

Planks strengthen your abs and help your posture, so your tummy looks firmer. But tummy fat can't be removed from one spot only. It goes down when your whole body loses fat through eating with portion control and moving more every day.

How long should I hold a plank?

For beginners, 15-30 seconds with good technique is already very good. Better 3 short, clean sets than 2 minutes with your hips dropping.

Can I plank every day?

Yes, if your body doesn't feel pain and you keep the dose moderate. But 3 times a week is enough to see strength progress.
