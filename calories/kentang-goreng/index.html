Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, but fries are easy to overeat because they're tasty and don't keep you full long. A medium size is already close to more than a cup of rice. If you want some, get a small or share. Make sure there's protein in the same meal.

3 smarter tips

  • Pick small, or share a medium.
  • Don't upsize your set, even if it's just a bit extra.
  • Eat your burger or chicken first, then the fries, so you're less likely to overeat.

FAQ

Are air fryer fries better?

Air fryers use less oil, so the fat is lower. The texture is a bit different but still crispy.

Why are fast food fries so crispy?

They're usually fried twice and lightly coated with a bit of flour or sugar. That keeps them crispy.

Can cold fries be reheated?

Yes, use an air fryer or a dry pan for a few minutes. Avoid the microwave.
