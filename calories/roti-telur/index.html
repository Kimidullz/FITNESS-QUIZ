How many calories in Roti telur?

One piece of roti telur has an estimated 400 kcal, not including kuah. Most calories come from the roti canai dough and oil, while the egg adds protein.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, and roti telur is actually a better choice than two pieces of roti kosong. The egg gives protein that helps you feel full. One piece with kuah dal and a kosong drink is enough for breakfast. Just don't make it your daily menu every morning.

3 smarter tips

  • Choose one roti telur over two roti kosong.
  • Take kuah dal and avoid kuah kari with santan.
  • Add a boiled egg or have warm water, not a sweet drink.

FAQ

Roti telur or roti canai kosong, which is better for diet?

One roti telur keeps you fuller than one roti kosong because of the protein. This helps you avoid snacking early.

Is roti double telur worth it?

If you want more protein, sure. But calories go up by about 80 kcal, so count it in your daily budget.

Can I ask for roti telur with less oil?

You can try asking the roti man to use less oil when cooking. Some shops are okay with it, especially if you're a regular.
