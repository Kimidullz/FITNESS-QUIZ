How many calories in Almonds?

1 handful of almonds (30 g) has about 170 kcal. Most of the calories come from fat, mainly unsaturated fat.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, almonds are a good snack because they have healthy fat, fibre and some protein. But they're calorie-dense. Eating straight from the jar, you can easily finish 300-500 kcal without noticing. The key is portion.

3 smarter tips

  • Pack almonds into small 30 g containers for the week and bring them to the office.
  • Choose almonds without salt or coating.
  • Pair with fruit like an apple for a more filling snack.

FAQ

How many almonds are in 30 g?

About 20-23 almonds, depending on size.

Raw or roasted almonds, which is better?

Both are okay. Choose roasted ones without too much oil and salt.

Are almonds okay as a night snack?

Can, as long as you stick to the portion. A small handful is enough to ease hunger.
