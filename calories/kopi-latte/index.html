How many calories in Caffe latte?

A 350 ml cup of latte has about 190 kcal (estimate). Most of it comes from full cream fresh milk, not the coffee.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes. Latte is actually a fairly okay coffee choice. The milk gives some protein and helps you feel full. What pushes the calories up is flavoured syrup, whipped cream and large sizes. Stick to a regular size and skip the syrup, and a latte fits into your diet easily.

3 smarter tips

  • Ask for low-fat or skim milk if they have it.
  • Skip the flavoured syrup, or ask for just half a pump.
  • Choose a small or medium size, not large.

FAQ

What's the difference between a latte and a cappuccino?

A latte has more milk and a little foam. A cappuccino has less milk and more foam, so it's usually lower in calories.

How much caffeine is in a latte?

It depends on the number of espresso shots. Most cafes use 1-2 shots for a regular size.

Is oat milk healthier than normal milk?

Not necessarily. Oat milk has about the same calories but less protein. Choose based on taste and needs, for example if you're lactose intolerant.
