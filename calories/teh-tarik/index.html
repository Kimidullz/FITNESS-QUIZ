How many calories in Teh tarik?

One glass of mamak teh tarik has about 120 kcal (estimate). Most calories come from the condensed milk, not the tea itself.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, teh tarik can still fit into a weight loss plan. One glass a day is no big deal if your daily calories are under control. What people often miss is that 2-3 glasses a day can add 300+ kcal without making you full. So control how often, no need to ban it completely.

3 smarter tips

  • Order 'teh tarik kurang manis' and save about 40 kcal straight away.
  • Pick a small glass or hot cup, not the big iced glass.
  • If you already had teh tarik in the morning, go for plain water or teh O kosong for your next drink.

FAQ

What's the difference between teh tarik and regular milk tea?

Teh tarik is 'pulled' from one jug to another until it's frothy. The base is the same: strong tea and condensed milk.

Does teh tarik have a lot of caffeine?

Yes, about the same or a bit less than a cup of coffee. If you're sensitive to caffeine, avoid it late in the afternoon.

Can I ask for teh tarik with evaporated milk only?

Yes, most mamak understand if you ask for 'teh C'. Less sweet but still creamy.
