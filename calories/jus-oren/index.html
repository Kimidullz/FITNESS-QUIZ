How many calories in Orange juice?

A 250 ml packet of orange juice has about 110 kcal (estimate). Almost all of it comes from sugar, either natural fruit sugar or added sugar.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, but don't treat juice the same as eating fruit. Juice has little fibre, so it's less filling and easy to drink a lot. One packet once in a while is okay. If you want to lose weight, eating a real orange usually helps more.

3 smarter tips

  • Read the label: choose '100% juice' and avoid 'flavoured drink'.
  • Mix half juice with half cold water for a lighter taste.
  • Eat one orange and drink plain water instead. More filling, fewer calories.

FAQ

Does packet orange juice have vitamin C?

Yes, usually quite a lot. But a fresh orange also gives vitamin C, plus fibre.

What's the difference between 100% orange juice and an orange drink?

100% juice is made only from fruit juice. Orange drinks usually have water, sugar and flavouring, with a low percentage of juice.

Can I give my kids packet orange juice every day?

Once in a while is okay. For daily drinks, plain water, milk and fresh fruit are better because juice is still high in sugar.
