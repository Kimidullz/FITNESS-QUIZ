How many calories in Granola with yogurt?

1 bowl of granola with yogurt is about 380 kcal (estimate). Most of the calories come from the granola, which is baked with oil, honey and nuts.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, but don't think of granola as diet food. Granola is calorie-dense, a spoon or two already adds up. Yogurt gives you good protein. If you watch your granola portion, this is a filling and tasty breakfast.

3 smarter tips

  • Use granola as a topping, not the main part of the bowl.
  • Choose plain or Greek yogurt, avoid sweet flavoured ones.
  • Add fresh fruit for natural sweetness and skip the honey.

FAQ

What's the difference between granola and muesli?

Granola is baked with oil and sweetener, so it's crunchy. Muesli is usually raw and not baked, often with less sugar.

Greek yogurt or regular yogurt?

Greek yogurt is thicker and has almost double the protein. Regular yogurt is okay too, just pick plain.

Can I make my own granola?

Yes. Bake oats with a little oil, honey and nuts in the oven. It's easy to control the sugar and oil.
