Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, one of the best choices when eating out. Moderate calories with some protein. The soup also keeps you full longer. Just watch the sodium in the broth if you bloat easily.

3 smarter tips

  • Ask for extra chicken or fish balls for more protein.
  • Add sawi or bean sprouts if the stall has them.
  • No need to finish all the soup if it's too salty.

FAQ

Is kuey teow sup good when you have a fever?

Many people like it because the soup is warm and easy to swallow. But if you're sick, follow your doctor's advice, okay.

Kuey teow or mee hoon in soup, which is better?

Both are okay. Mee hoon is slightly lighter, while kuey teow feels more filling for some people.

Can I eat kuey teow sup at night?

Yes. It's light and easy to digest, good for a late dinner.
