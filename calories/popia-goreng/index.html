Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, if you watch how many. Two rolls are still okay as a snack. The problem is mini popia are easy to eat by the dozen during buka puasa or at events. Count how many before you start eating.

3 smarter tips

  • Take 2 rolls and put them on a plate. Don't eat straight from the container.
  • Pick popia basah if available, because it's not fried.
  • At home, use an air fryer and add more vegetables to the filling.

FAQ

What's the usual popia goreng filling?

Sengkuang, carrot, taugeh and sometimes dried prawns or minced chicken.

How do I stop popia goreng from being oily?

Make sure the filling is dry and the oil is hot. Drain on a rack or kitchen towel after frying.

Can I freeze popia goreng?

Yes, freeze them before frying. Fry straight from frozen, no need to thaw.
