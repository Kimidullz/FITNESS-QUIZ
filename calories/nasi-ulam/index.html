How many calories in Nasi ulam?

A plate of nasi ulam has about 400 kcal. Most of the calories come from the rice and kerisik. The ulam itself is very low in calories.

Can I eat it while losing weight?

Yes, nasi ulam is one of the lightest rice options. Ulam gives fibre and flavour without many calories. But protein is quite low if there's only a bit of fish. Add a grilled or boiled side for a more balanced meal.

3 smarter tips

  • Ask for more ulam and slightly less rice.
  • Add ikan bakar or a boiled egg for protein.
  • Take just a little kerisik, it's dense in fat.

FAQ

What ulam is usually in nasi ulam?

Daun kesum, pegaga, daun kaduk, serai, bunga kantan and daun limau purut. Every state has its own mix.

What's the difference between nasi ulam and nasi kerabu?

Nasi kerabu is usually blue rice with ulam served separately and a sauce. In nasi ulam, the ulam is mixed straight into the rice.

Can I make nasi ulam at home?

Yes. Slice the ulam finely, then mix with cold rice, kerisik, fish and salt. Easy and fast.
