**Why it should work:** It's an inside-view format with a relatable Monday-scale panic and a calming, specific number.

**Hook options:**
1. +1.5kg after the weekend? Most of it isn't fat
2. Water weight vs fat gain: what your Monday scale isn't telling you
3. To gain 1.5kg of fat in 2 days, you'd need ~11,000 extra kcal

**Slides:**
1. **+1.5kg after the weekend? Most of it isn't fat**
   Here's what is happening inside your body from Saturday to Monday.
2. **Carbs hold water**
   Your body stores carbs with water, roughly 3g of water for every 1g of carbs. More rice and kuih on the weekend means more water on the scale.
3. **Salt holds water too**
   Kuah, sambal, keropok, kicap. More salt means your body holds on to more water for a few days.
4. **Food is still in your gut**
   A big Sunday dinner still has weight on Monday morning. That isn't fat. It's just food not yet digested.
5. **Real fat gain is much slower**
   1kg of fat is about 7,700 kcal. To gain 1.5kg of fat in 2 days, you'd need ~11,000 kcal above what you burn. Most weekends aren't that.
6. **Don't panic. Get back to normal.**
   Drink water, eat normal meals, take a walk. The scale usually settles in a few days. Check your weekly average. Take my 2-minute quiz, link in bio.

**Caption:**
Monday scale shock? 😅 Most of that jump is water, salt and food still in your gut, not fat.
