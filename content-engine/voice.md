## Audience
Malaysians who want to lose fat: office workers, shift workers, mums, people who eat at the mamak, kedai nasi campur and pasar. Budget-aware. Many are beginners.

## Language basics
- Simple words, short sentences. Malaysian food names stay in Malay (nasi lemak, teh tarik, kuih, tauhu, ikan kembung, lauk, pasar, kedai, mamak).
- Friendly coach talking to a friend. Not preachy, no shaming, no "you are lazy".
- No foods are banned. Swaps and portions, not cutting everything.

## What flops
Talking-head videos, generic motivation, "uncomfortable truths", "I rated your excuses", hashtag-heavy captions, DM START / DM MULA CTAs, posts with no number.

## Language
- IG whiteboard reels: Malay (both viral reels were Malay). Casual "korang/you" Malay, English words where Malaysians use them.
- TikTok carousels: English.

## Rules for every post
- Numbers are approximate and say so ("~", "approx.", "prices vary by area"). Round down. Never make up a study; if unsure, say "about" and keep it general.
- No medical claims, no "cure", no supplement brand names (no Herbalife on public posts). No phone numbers.
- No emoji on slides. One or two emoji in captions is fine.
- Carousels are 4:5 (IG size), 6 slides: hook, 4 content slides, CTA. Same images go to TikTok.
- Captions: short, end with a question about the viewer's own habit + a pinned comment. Few or no hashtags.
- Soft CTA: "Take my 2-minute quiz, link in bio" or "Save this", not hard selling.
- Keep reels gentle in pace (older viewers too). Never put song lyrics on screen.

## Example captions in my voice
> Your RM8 protein bar has about the same protein as RM1.50 of eggs 🥚
> I worked out what 20g of protein costs in Malaysia. Whey is convenient, not magic. Food first.
> Prices are approximate and vary by area. What's an egg in your area? 👇

> Your 4pm kuih, ranked by calories 🍩
> Kuih isn't the problem. 3 kuih and a teh tarik is ~650 kcal, more than a plate of nasi lemak. Pick 1 and have teh O kosong.
> Numbers are approximate, sizes vary by shop. Which kuih can you never say no to? 👇

> You can eat clean and train hard, and still struggle to lose fat if you sleep 5 hours a night.
> Sleep is part of your diet. Aim for 7 to 9 hours.

> "Just a little" but every day 🥤 1 teh tarik is ~150 kcal. Times 30 days is ~4,500 kcal, about 0.5kg a month. Don't stop. Swap.
