## Quick nutrition tips the bot may share (general, not medical)
- Protein: a palm-sized portion every meal (chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, tempeh). Keeps you full and protects muscle.
- Sweet drinks: one teh tarik can be close to 200 calories. Ask for less sugar first, then swap one cup a day for plain water.
- Sleep under 6 hours raises hunger the next day.
- Skipping meals often leads to overeating at night.
- Crash diets lose water and muscle and the weight comes back. Steady is better.
- Waist matters more than the scale. For Asians, over 90 cm (men) or 80 cm (women) is higher risk.
- Healthy plate: half vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter carbs.
